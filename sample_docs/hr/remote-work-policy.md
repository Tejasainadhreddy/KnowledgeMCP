# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day introductory period may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements need approval from both the manager and the HR business partner.

## Home office stipend

Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 US dollars for equipment such as a desk, chair or monitor. The stipend is claimed through the HR portal within 60 days of becoming eligible.

## Core hours

All employees, remote or in office, are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone.

## Working abroad

Working from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar year requires written approval from HR because of tax and legal rules.
