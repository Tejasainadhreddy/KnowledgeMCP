# Paid Time Off Policy

## Vacation

Full-time employees earn 20 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Days above 5 are forfeited on January 1.

Vacation requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your manager.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Company holidays

Northwind Labs observes 11 paid company holidays each year. The list is published in the HR portal every December for the following year.

## Parental leave

Birth and non-birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
