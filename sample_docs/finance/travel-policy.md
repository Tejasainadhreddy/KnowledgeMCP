# Travel Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Flights must be economy class for trips shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer with VP approval.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 250 US dollars in high-cost cities such as San Francisco and New York, and 175 US dollars everywhere else.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance during travel is 75 US dollars. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Ground transport

Use public transit or ride share when practical. Rental cars need manager approval and must be the compact class or smaller unless there are 3 or more travellers.
